# Inbox

Drop links, half-formed thoughts, or corrections to your own framing under
**Open**. The next sweep reads this first: each link is assessed against the
thesis, each note feeds the course check, and the entry moves to **Processed**
with a line on what was done with it.

You can also answer the questions the dashboard asks you (Now → "questions only
you can answer"). The answers change what the sweeps look for.
